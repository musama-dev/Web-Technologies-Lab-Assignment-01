About the Symposium

The Annual Computer Science Symposium is a premier event held on 12 March 2026 at COMSATS University, Sahiwal Campus, where students, teachers, and technology professionals come together to discuss modern trends in computer science.

Why Attend?

  1. Learn about new technologies.
  2. Network with students and professionals.
  3. Explore computer science trends.

Quick Information

  • Entry is free for all students.
  • Registration closes on 5 March 2026.
  • Certificates will be given to all participants.
